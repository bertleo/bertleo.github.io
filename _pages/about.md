## About Me

I am a Project Officer at the European Commission's Joint Research Centre (JRC), where I conduct research on climate risks in Europe and globally, with a focus on identifying and implementing blue and green solutions. My work develops dynamic scenarios that integrate multiple climate risks to assess the costs and benefits of adaptation and mitigation strategies, informing climate action policy and long-term environmental resilience.

Before joining the JRC, I was a Post-Doctoral Researcher at the University of Notre Dame, working on eco-hydrological modeling of wetland and river network dynamics, water stress from refugee migration, and socio-hydrological models of community resilience. Earlier, as a Post-Doctoral Researcher at Purdue University, I developed theoretical frameworks for species dispersal on stochastic, patchy habitat networks.

I hold a Ph.D. from Purdue University and an M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Environmental Engineering from the University of Padova.

## Research Interests

- Climate risk assessment and nature-based solutions
- Blue and green adaptation strategies for European cities
- Eco-hydrology and wetland dynamics
- Socio-hydrological resilience of human communities
