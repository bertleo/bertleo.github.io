Without the filtering performed by Europe's wetlands, the nitrogen pollution that rivers carry into the seas would be 25% higher. Restoring some of the historical wetlands would further cut nitrogen pollution with low impact on agriculture, based on findings from the [3-30-300 cooling paper](/publication/2025-01-01-the-important-role-of-wetland-conservation-and-restoration).
